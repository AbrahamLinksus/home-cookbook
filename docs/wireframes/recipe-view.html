Grandma's Apple Pie

★
Servings −4+
Scaled from 8 servings · Reset
Ingredients
  • 1 cup flour
  • ½ cup butter
  • 3 apples
  • ⅓ cup sugar
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • salt, to taste
Scaled amounts for viewing only; the saved recipe stays at 8 servings.
Steps
  1. Preheat oven to 200°C.
  2. Rub the butter into the flour and salt until crumbly.
  3. Peel, core and slice the apples; toss with sugar and cinnamon.
  4. Line the dish with pastry, fill with apples, cover with pastry.
  5. Bake 45 minutes until golden.
